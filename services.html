Services

Five things I build. Most clients start with one.

Finance automation

I connect your bank statements and operational spreadsheets directly to your accounting system, so bills, invoices and payments post themselves. Includes foreign exchange handling, tax treatment, duplicate protection and checks that refuse to post if source documents are missing. Everything runs in preview mode first, so you approve before anything touches your books.

Recruitment funnels

Hiring is where small companies lose the most time. I build application funnels that candidates reach by scanning a QR code on a poster, a job ad or a shop window. The form screens against your criteria automatically, so unqualified applicants are filtered out before a human ever reads them, and the ones who pass land in your task system ready for interview, already sorted. Your HR team stops reading two hundred applications and starts reading twelve.

Payroll and HR systems

Multi-location timesheet and payroll systems with your pay week, your holiday rules and your locations built in. Leave and vacation requests handled by form and routed automatically. Administrative controls so files lock down when payroll is being processed.

Records and reporting

Years of bank statements converted from PDF into structured, formula-driven workbooks, reconciled to the cent. Consolidated dashboards so leadership sees the position without asking anyone for it.

Fractional operations support

For companies that do not need a full-time operations hire. Ledger management, payroll runs, supplier and contractor coordination, governance and filings, property and tenant coordination, and executive support, on a monthly retainer.
